# Product Context

## Why This Site Exists

Hobart Motorcycles is a family-operated motorcycle store established in August 2016 and located south of Hobart, Tasmania. The website gives prospective and existing customers a simple way to learn about the business, browse available motorcycles, understand finance options, find contact information, and review sales and shipping policies.

## Problems It Solves

Customers need to be able to:

- Understand what Hobart Motorcycles offers and who operates it.
- Browse a motorcycle catalogue before contacting the store.
- Learn about finance, insurance, warranty, and roadside-assistance options.
- Find business contact and location information.
- Review shipping, returns, privacy, and sale terms.
- Navigate the site comfortably on desktop and mobile devices.

The repository also solves a maintenance problem: the original site was generated by Magento and then scraped into static files. The static copy needs to remain useful even though server-backed Magento features and some remote assets are unavailable or stale.

## Intended User Experience

- The site should feel like the established Hobart Motorcycles brand, not a generic replacement.
- Key routes should be discoverable from a consistent header and mobile menu.
- Promotional images and catalogue content should load quickly and remain legible at common viewport sizes.
- Calls to action should lead to viable static-site actions such as phone, email, directions, or local content.
- Controls inherited from Magento must either work meaningfully or be removed/disabled clearly; users should not encounter fake ecommerce behavior.
- Policy content should be readable and directly navigable.
- External destinations such as social media or legitimate third-party services should remain intentional and clearly external.

## Product Constraints

- This is an informational/static catalogue experience, not a functioning online store.
- Content is embedded in HTML and must be updated manually.
- Enquiries require a static-compatible method or an external service; there is no repository backend.
- The static deployment must not depend on the legacy production site for essential presentation or navigation.

## Content and Brand Notes

Confirmed repository content includes business history, brand/partner imagery, motorcycle listings, finance information, contact details, and policies. Existing wording and legal content should be preserved unless the owner explicitly requests or approves copy changes. Legal and business details must not be inferred or silently modernized.
